# DIY Home Wiring Backup Wire Guide

This guide helps you choose the right **mm² wire sizes** to keep as backup for common home electrical work.

---

## 🔌 Recommended Wire Sizes to Keep

### **1.0 mm² Copper Wire**

- For: LED lights, bulbs, small lighting circuits
- Why: Low current draw, flexible
- Keep: 10–20 meters

### **1.5 mm² Copper Wire**

- For: Lights + ceiling fans
- Why: Standard for most lighting + fan points
- Keep: 20–30 meters

### **2.5 mm² Copper Wire**

- For: 6A/10A power sockets (TV, mixer, laptop, router)
- Why: Handles more load than lighting wires
- Keep: 20–30 meters

### **4 mm² Copper Wire**

- For: High-load appliances (geyser, AC up to 1.5 Ton, washing machine)
- Keep: 10–15 meters

### **6 mm² Copper Wire**

- For: Main line feed, inverter wiring, heavy appliances
- Keep: 5–10 meters

---

## 🧰 General Guidelines

- Always use **ISI-marked copper wires**.
- Choose **FR/FRLS (Fire Resistant / Low Smoke)** type when possible.
- Buy a mix of **red, black, and green** for phase, neutral, and earth.
- Keep extra length (10–15%) for loops, box entry, and routing.

---

## 📝 Notes

- Avoid aluminum wires for home DIY work.
- Never mix wire sizes in the same circuit.
- Ensure MCB ratings match the wire size.
- Use proper connectors (Wagos or screw terminals).
