# 📚 Common Terms Used in Electrician Work

## ⚡ Basic Electrical Terms

- **Voltage (V):** Electrical pressure that pushes current through a circuit.
- **Current (A):** Flow of electric charge through a conductor.
- **Resistance (Ω):** Opposition to current flow.
- **Power (W):** Rate of energy consumption (Voltage × Current).
- **Load:** Any device that consumes electricity (fan, bulb, motor).
- **Phase (Live):** Wire that carries current from supply.
- **Neutral:** Returns current back to the source.
- **Earth (Ground):** Safety path to prevent electric shock.

## 🔌 Wiring & Cable Terms

- **Conductor:** Metal (usually copper) that carries current.
- **Insulation:** Protective covering on a wire.
- **Cable Sheath:** Outer layer on multi-core cables.
- **Single-Core Wire:** One conductor—used in home wiring.
- **Stranded Wire:** Multiple small copper strands—flexible.
- **Gauge (AWG):** Wire thickness measurement (US system).
- **Cross-Sectional Area (mm²):** Wire size used in India/EU.

## 🛠️ Electrical Component Terms

- **Switch:** Device to turn load ON/OFF.
- **Socket/Outlet:** Point to connect appliances.
- **MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker):** Protects from overload/short circuit.
- **RCD/ELCB/RCCB:** Protects from earth leakage and electrocution.
- **Distribution Board (DB):** Panel containing breakers and wiring.
- **Busbar:** Metal strip for distributing power within DB.
- **Junction Box:** Enclosure where wires join.
- **Terminal Block / Wago:** Safe connector for joining wires.
- **Conduit:** Pipe that carries wires safely.

## 🔧 Measurement & Testing Terms

- **Multimeter:** Measures voltage, current, resistance.
- **Clamp Meter:** Measures current without breaking circuit.
- **Continuity Test:** Checks if a wire is broken.
- **Polarity Test:** Ensures Live/Neutral connections are correct.
- **Insulation Resistance Test:** Checks wire insulation quality.
- **Voltage Tester (Pen):** Detects live wires without contact.

## 🔥 Safety Terms

- **Earthing/Grounding:** Prevents shocks by connecting metal parts to earth.
- **Short Circuit:** Live wire touches Neutral/Earth directly.
- **Overload:** Too much current through a circuit.
- **Arc Fault:** Electrical discharge caused by loose connections.
- **Trip:** When a breaker switches off automatically.
- **Shock Hazard:** Risk of current passing through the human body.

## ⚙️ Appliance & System Terms

- **AC Supply:** Alternating current (home electricity).
- **DC Supply:** Direct current (batteries, solar).
- **Inverter:** Converts DC to AC.
- **UPS:** Provides backup power.
- **Earthing Pit:** Grounding system for a building.
- **Load Calculation:** Determining total electricity needs.

## ⚡ Project & Installation Terms

- **Wiring Diagram:** Visual representation of electrical circuits.
- **Circuit Looping:** Taking power from one point to another.
- **Parallel Connection:** Keeps voltage constant across loads.
- **Series Connection:** Voltage shared—used for certain circuits.
- **Splicing:** Joining wires.
- **Lug:** Metal terminal for connecting thick wires.
- **Ferrule:** A sleeve used for clean terminal connections.

## 📏 Electrical Standards & Ratings

- **Wattage Rating:** Maximum load a device can handle.
- **IP Rating:** Dust/water protection for electrical devices.
- **Breaker Rating (Amp):** Current limit of an MCB.
- **Wire Rating:** Maximum current a wire can safely carry.
- **ISI / CE Standards:** Safety certifications for electrical products.
