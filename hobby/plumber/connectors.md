|----|---------------------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------:|

| 8  | **Flange / Closet Flange**      |          A bolted plate for connecting pipes to fixtures (e.g., securing a toilet).           |   <img src="images/plumbing/08-flange-closet-flange.jpg" width="40">    |

| 11 | **Water Meter**                 |              Measures the volume of water passing through the main service line.              |        <img src="images/plumbing/11-water-meter.jpg" width="40">        |
| 12 | **Wye (Y-fitting)**             |   A branch fitting, crucial for sewage, that joins pipes at a less abrupt angle than a Tee.   |       <img src="images/plumbing/12-wye-y-fitting.jpg" width="40">       |
| 13 | **Cleanout**                    |            An accessible opening installed in a drain line for removing blockages.            |         <img src="images/plumbing/13-cleanout.jpg" width="40">          |
| 14 | **Copper Soldering**            |             Joining copper pipes using heat, solder, and flux (capillary action).             |     <img src="images/plumbing/14-copper-soldering.jpg" width="40">      |
| 15 | **Solvent Cement (PVC/CPVC)**   |           Chemical process using primer and cement to weld plastic pipes together.            |  <img src="images/plumbing/15-solvent-cement-pvc-cpvc.jpg" width="40">  |
| 16 | **PEX Crimp Ring / Clamp**      |      Mechanical connection used to join PEX piping with fittings (ring or clamp style).       |   <img src="images/plumbing/16-pex-crimp-ring-clamp.jpg" width="40">    |
| 17 | **Push-Fit / SharkBite**        |       A fitting that snaps onto a pipe (Copper, PEX, CPVC) for a tool-free connection.        |    <img src="images/plumbing/17-push-fit-sharkbite.jpg" width="40">     |
| 18 | **Compression Fitting**         | Uses a nut and ferrule (ring) to compress onto a pipe, creating a seal (common for fixtures). |    <img src="images/plumbing/18-compression-fitting.jpg" width="40">    |
| 19 | **Adapter (Male/Female)**       |           Converts a connection type (e.g., threaded to slip/glue, or FIP to MIP).            |    <img src="images/plumbing/19-adapter-male-female.jpg" width="40">    |
| 20 | **Trap Primer**                 |     A device that automatically adds water to a floor drain P-trap to maintain the seal.      |        <img src="images/plumbing/20-trap-primer.jpg" width="40">        |
| 21 | **Drainage Floor Sink**         |         A large basin in a floor, often used in commercial kitchens to catch runoff.          |    <img src="images/plumbing/21-drainage-floor-sink.jpg" width="40">    |

## Usage notes

- Filenames in `images/plumbing/` should match the names above (lowercase, hyphens, no spaces).
- The fittings listed cover both supply lines (Copper, PEX) and drainage systems (PVC/DWV).
